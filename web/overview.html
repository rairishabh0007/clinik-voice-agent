How it works

Riya, an AI care coordinator, calls a patient about their lab results and tries to book a doctor's appointment. After the call, everything is analysed and saved in Opik.

One call, step by step

  1. 1
    Start the call You pick a patient. Patients who opted out of calls are blocked here.
  2. 2
    Check who is speaking Riya confirms she is talking to the right person before sharing anything.
  3. 3
    Share the results She reads the patient's HbA1c and glucose values exactly as given, with the normal range.
  4. 4
    Book an appointment She finds a free slot with a tool call and books it. If the slot is taken, she offers other times.
  5. 5
    Analyse and save When the call ends, an LLM writes a summary and outcome, and the call is sent to Opik with its scores.

Saved in Opik for every call

  • Details — patient, models used, call duration
  • Transcript — every turn of the conversation
  • Recording — the call audio
  • Tool calls — identity check, slot search, booking
  • Analysis — outcome, booked or not, summary, next step
  • Scores — medical safety and booking evaluations

Safety rules Riya follows

  • No health details until identity is confirmed
  • Only the numbers she was given, never made up
  • No diagnosis or medicine advice — that is for the doctor
  • Urgent symptoms: tells them to get medical help and hands over to a human
  • Voicemail: leaves a callback message only, no results
  • "Don't call me again" is respected and recorded

What runs where

LiveKitConnects the call and runs the agent
GeminiRiya's replies and the post-call analysis
DeepgramTurns speech into text and text into speech
OpikStores each call and scores it
